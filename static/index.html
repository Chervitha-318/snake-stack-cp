Rules to Play:

1.The head of the snake continually moves forward, unable to stop, growing longer.
2.Snake can be controlled using arrow keys or W/S/A/D keys.
3.Snake cannot reverse direction instantly.
4.Food appears at random empty cells when consumed.
5.Food Types: - Carrot: (length +1 & score +1) - Pumpkin : (length +3 & score +3) - Golden Apple: (immunity for 10 seconds).
6.If not immune, the snake can die by: hitting a wall, or colliding with its own body.
7.When immune: -The snake wraps around walls instead of dying -On self-collision, the game pauses until a key is pressed.
8.The game starts when the player presses a movement key.
9.The game ends immediately upon collision(if not immune).
NOTE: Score = health = length of snake
