Rules to Play:

1.The head of the snake continually moves forward, unable to stop, growing longer.
2.Snake can be steered up,down,left,right by either using arrow keys or by using WSAD respectively.
3.Food appears at random empty cells when the snake eats food.
4.Carrot (health +1); Pumpkin Pie (health +3); Golden Apple (immunity for 10 seconds).
5.If not immune, the snake can die by: hitting a wall, or colliding with its own body.
NOTE: Score = health = length of snake
